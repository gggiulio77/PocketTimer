Hands-free pocket timer. Pause+play headphones to start. Works offline, locked.


FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 characters max)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PocketTimer is a simple, reliable countdown timer designed to stay in your pocket. Start it hands-free with a quick pause-then-play on your headphones or any media controller - no need to unlock your phone or leave your workout, kitchen, or focus session.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set minutes and seconds with smooth scroll pickers.
2. Grant Notification/Media Observer and Exact Alarm access (buttons turn green when ready).
3. Play music or a podcast as usual.
4. Pause, then play again within one second - the timer starts automatically.
5. When time is up, a clear beep lets you know.

WHY POCKET TIMER
- Hands-free start - phone stays locked in your pocket
- Works with Spotify, YouTube Music, podcasts, and other media apps
- Smart detection - ignores track skips and volume changes to avoid false starts
- Reliable countdown - keeps running with the screen off via a foreground service
- Exact timing - uses exact alarms so the end time stays accurate in battery-saving modes
- Live notification - see remaining time and cancel anytime from the notification
- Remembers your last duration for faster setup next time

PRIVACY-FRIENDLY
- Fully offline - no internet permission required
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs
- No accounts and no personal data collection
- Only stores timer preferences on your device

BEST FOR
Workouts and intervals, cooking and kitchen timers, study focus sessions, walks, chores, and any moment you want a timer without touching the screen.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 12 (API 31) or higher. Optional Notification access enables the hands-free media gesture; you can still start the timer manually from the app.
