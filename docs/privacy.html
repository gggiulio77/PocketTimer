Privacy Policy

PocketTimer (package: com.gonzalogiulio.pockettimer)
Developer: Gonzalo Giulio
Effective date: July 23, 2026

PocketTimer is a local countdown timer for Android with an optional hands-free start gesture (a quick pause-then-play on media controls such as headphones). This policy explains what data the app uses, what it does not collect, and why certain permissions are requested.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • The app works fully offline and does not require internet access.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • Only a few timer-related preferences are stored on your device.

2. Data we do not collect

PocketTimer does not collect or transmit:

  • Name, email, phone number, or account information
  • Location, contacts, photos, files, or microphone/camera data
  • Advertising identifiers or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics to external servers
  • Payment or financial information

The app does not include a network permission and does not send data to our servers or to third parties.

3. Data stored only on your device

The app may store the following locally using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Last selected timer duration (minutes and seconds)
  • Internal flags used for correct app behavior (for example, whether the app is considered active, and the last timer finish time to avoid accidental restarts)

This information stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it, subject to any restore from an Android system backup (see below).

4. Permissions and why we use them

PocketTimer requests only the capabilities needed to run a reliable timer and optional hands-free media control. None of these permissions are used to collect or upload personal data.

Permission / access Purpose
Notifications Show the ongoing timer countdown and status while the timer is running.
Foreground service (media playback type) Keep the timer running reliably when the screen is off or the app is in the background, and play the completion beep sound.
Exact alarms Fire the timer at the correct end time, including when battery-saving modes are active.
Wake lock Briefly keep the device awake so the completion beep can play reliably.
Notification access (Notification Listener) Observe active media playback sessions (for example, music or podcast apps) so a quick pause-then-play on headphones or other media controls can start the timer without unlocking the phone. See the next section for details.

5. Notification access and media information

To support hands-free control, you may optionally enable Notification access for PocketTimer in Android system settings. This is a sensitive system permission.

When enabled, the app uses it to:

  • Access media session playback state (playing, paused, position)
  • Temporarily read limited media metadata in memory (such as track identifiers, title, or duration) only to distinguish an intentional pause-then-play gesture from track changes or other media events

We do not:

  • Store media metadata or playback history on disk
  • Send media information or notification content off your device
  • Use notification access for advertising or profiling

The app’s code does not process general notification text for other apps. Enabling Notification access is still a system-level capability; you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings. The timer can still be started manually from the app without this access.

6. No internet and no sale of data

PocketTimer does not request internet permission and does not transmit user data over the network. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties because the app does not collect such data.

7. Backups

If Android Auto Backup (or a similar system backup feature) is enabled on your device, the operating system may include the app’s local preferences in your personal device backup. PocketTimer does not operate its own cloud sync or account-based backup.

8. Children

PocketTimer is a general utility timer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no special children’s data processing occurs.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app’s behavior changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
